# Kestron AI

**Production AI infrastructure and DevOps on AWS, for SaaS teams in the US and UK.**

We help Series A and B SaaS companies run reliable, secure and cost-efficient cloud and AI infrastructure, without hiring a full platform team.

## What we do

- **AI infrastructure on AWS**: Amazon Bedrock, RAG pipelines, vector databases and LLM cost control
- **DevOps and platform engineering**: CI/CD, Terraform, Kubernetes (EKS), monitoring and on-call
- **Security and compliance**: least-privilege IAM, secrets management and SOC 2 evidence
- **Cloud cost reviews**: finding and fixing waste in AWS and LLM spend

## How we work

- Everything is built as code (Terraform), reviewed and documented
- Work happens in your own cloud accounts and repositories
- Clear handover: runbooks and docs your team keeps
